ogcore.pensions.SS_amount(w, n, theta, t, j, shift, method, e, p)[source]#

Calculate public pension benefit amounts for each household under a US-style social security system.

\[pension_{j,s,t} = \theta_j \times w_t \quad \forall s \geq R_{t-s}\]

where \(R_{t-s}\) is the retirement age of the cohort born in period \(t - s\), read from the (T + S) x S array p.retire.

Parameters:

  • w (array_like) – real wage rate

  • n (Numpy array) – labor supply

  • theta (array_like) – social security replacement rate before replacement_rate_adjust scaling. A length J vector when j is None; a scalar (or length 1 array) for group j when n is 1D or method is ‘TPI_scalar’

  • t (int) – time period

  • j (int) – index of lifetime income group

  • shift (bool) – whether computing for periods 0–s or 1–(s+1), =True for 1–(s+1)

  • method (str) – adjusts calculation dimensions based on ‘SS’ or ‘TPI’

  • e (Numpy array) – effective labor units

  • p (OG-Core Specifications object) – model parameters

Returns:

pension amount for each household

Return type:

pension (Numpy array)

Notes

replacement_rate_adjust is applied here, and only here, for the US-style system, so callers pass the unadjusted theta. Along the time path the factor is indexed by the year the benefit is paid and the retirement age by the household’s own cohort, so the single-cohort path agrees with the (T, S, J) array that the aggregates use. The steady state uses the final values of both.

ogcore.pensions.DB_amount(w, e, n, t, j, method, p)[source]#

Calculate public pension from a defined benefits system.

\[pension_{j,s,t} = \biggl[\frac{\sum_{u=R-ny}^{R-1}w_{t-s+u} e_{j,u,t-s+u}n_{j,u,t-s+u}}{ny}\biggr]\times Cy \times \alpha^{DB}_{j,s,t} \quad \forall s \geq R\]

where the retirement age \(R\), the averaging window \(ny\) (avg_earn_num_years), the years of contribution \(Cy\) (yr_contrib), and the replacement rate \(\alpha^{DB}\) (alpha_db) are those of the household’s own birth cohort at the age and period the benefit is paid, read from the period-by-age arrays that pension_regimes builds. With no regimes, \(R\), \(ny\), and \(Cy\) are the same for everyone and \(\alpha^{DB}_{j,t}\) varies only by lifetime income group and the calendar year of payment.

Parameters:

  • w (array_like) – real wage rate

  • e (Numpy array) – effective labor units

  • n (Numpy array) – labor supply; may be a partial lifetime path (length < S, for cohorts alive when the time path begins), a steady-state vector (S,) or matrix (S, J), or the full time path (T, S, J)

  • t (int) – model period of the first element of n (for a partial or full lifetime path) or of the first row of n (for the full time path)

  • j (int) – index of lifetime income group, None if all groups

  • method (str) – adjusts calculation dimensions based on ‘SS’, ‘TPI’, or ‘TPI_scalar’

  • p (OG-Core Specifications object) – model parameters

Returns:

pension amount for each household

Return type:

DB (Numpy array)

ogcore.pensions.NDC_amount(w, e, n, r, ndc_rates, t, j, method, p)[source]#

Calculate public pension from a notional defined contribution system.

\[pension_{j,s,t} = \biggl[\sum_{u=E}^{R-1}\tau^{p}_{u,t-s+u} w_{t-s+u}e_{j,u,t-s+u}n_{j,u,t-s+u} \prod_{a=u+1}^{R-1}(1 + g_{NDC,t-s+a}) \biggr]\delta_{R,s,t} \quad \forall s \geq R\]

where the retirement age \(R\) and the conversion coefficient \(\delta_{R,s,t}\) (through k_ret and indR) are those of the household’s own birth cohort at the age and period the benefit is paid, and the contribution rate \(\tau^{p}_{u,t-s+u}\) is the one the cohort faced at each working age u, all read from the period-by-age arrays that pension_regimes builds. Each contribution earns the rate credited in each period between the contribution and retirement, \(g_{NDC,t}\), and the conversion coefficient discounts payments at the rate in the period the household retires.

Parameters:

  • w (array_like) – real wage rate

  • e (Numpy array) – effective labor units

  • r (array_like) – interest rate, used for the steady-state rates when ndc_rates is not given

  • ndc_rates (NDCRates) – calendar paths of the credited and discount rates from ndc_rates_path; anything else (such as None) uses the steady-state rates, with the ‘r’ options reading the last element of r

  • t (int) – model period of the first element of n (for a partial or full lifetime path) or of the first row of n (for the full time path)

  • j (int) – index of lifetime income group, None if all groups

  • method (str) – adjusts calculation dimensions based on ‘SS’, ‘TPI’, or ‘TPI_scalar’

  • p (OG-Core Specifications object) – model parameters

Returns:

pension amount for each household

Return type:

ogcore.pensions.growth_rate_path(choice, r, Y, w, L, p, method)[source]#

Calendar path of an NDC growth rate.

Element t is the growth realized from period t - 1 to period t, in real, non-stationarized terms. Stationarized aggregates are divided by \(e^{g_y t}\) and by population, and p.g_n[t] is population growth from t to t + 1, so for an aggregate X

\[g_{t} = \frac{\hat{X}_{t}}{\hat{X}_{t-1}}e^{g_y} (1 + g_{n,t-1}) - 1\]

where X is GDP for ‘Curr GDP’ and the wage bill wL for ‘wage bill’. ‘avg wage’ is the growth of earnings per person, the same expression without population growth. ‘r’ is the interest rate path and ‘LR GDP’ the constant steady-state growth of GDP. Growth into period 0 uses the period-0 level as the level of the period before.

Parameters:

  • choice (str) – one of NDC_GROWTH_CHOICES

  • r (array_like) – interest rate path

  • Y (array_like) – GDP path, used for ‘Curr GDP’

  • w (array_like) – real wage path, used for ‘wage bill’ and ‘avg wage’

  • L (array_like) – aggregate labor path, used for ‘wage bill’ and ‘avg wage’

  • p (OG-Core Specifications object) – model parameters

  • method (str) – ‘SS’ for the steady-state value or ‘TPI’ for the time path

Returns:

length 1 in the steady state, else T + S

Return type:

rate (Numpy array)

ogcore.pensions.delta_ret(r, Y, p, S_ret=None, indR=None, k_ret=None, t_ret=None, g_dir_path=None)[source]#

Compute conversion coefficient for the NDC pension amount

\[\delta_{R} = (dir_{R} + ind_{R} - k)^{-1}\]

The mortality rates entering \(dir_{R}\) are set by the parameter ndc_mortality:

  • ‘steady state’: the steady-state mortality rates, for every cohort

  • ‘period’: the mortality rates by age in the period in which the household retires (a period life table)

  • ‘cohort’: the mortality rates the household’s own birth cohort faces at each age after retirement (a projected cohort life table), the rates that also enter the household’s problem

Payments are discounted at dir_growth_rate. Given a calendar path of that rate (g_dir_path), each household uses its value in the period it retires, held fixed over the payments; otherwise the steady-state rate is used.

Parameters:

  • r (array_like) – interest rate

  • Y (array_like) – GDP

  • p (OG-Core Specifications object) – model parameters

  • S_ret (array_like) – model period of retirement; any shape, or None for the steady-state value (the final row of retire)

  • indR (array_like) – survivor benefit adjustment, broadcastable against S_ret, or None for the steady-state value

  • k_ret (array_like) – payment frequency adjustment, broadcastable against S_ret, or None for the steady-state value

  • t_ret (array_like) – model period in which the household reaches age S_ret, broadcastable against S_ret; may lie before 0 or after T + S - 1, in which case the first or last row of the mortality rates is used. None for the steady state.

  • g_dir_path (array_like) – calendar path of the discount rate (NDCRates.discount), read at t_ret (clipped to the path); None to use the steady-state rate

Returns:

conversion coefficient for

the NDC pension amount, shaped like S_ret

Return type:

delta_ret (scalar or Numpy array)

ogcore.pensions.delta_ret_loop(S, S_ret, surv_rates, g_dir_value)[source]#

Compute the annuity factor in the NDC conversion coefficient

\[dir_{R} = \sum_{k=0}^{S-R-1}\left[\prod_{i=0}^{k-1} (1 - \hat{\rho}_{R+i})\right](1 + g_{dir})^{-k}\]

The pension is paid at ages R, …, S - 1. Following the household mortality convention, 1 - rho[s] is the probability of surviving from age s to age s + 1, so the payment at age R + k is received with probability prod_{i=0}^{k-1} (1 - rho[R + i]) by a household alive at retirement age R.

Parameters:

  • surv_rates (Numpy array) – length S survival rates by age, 1 - rho

  • g_dir_value (scalar) – discount rate used in the conversion coefficient

ogcore.pensions.DB_3dim_loop(w_path, e_ss, n, n_preTP, S_ret, S, J, g_y, avg_earn_num_years, alpha_db, yr_contr)[source]#

Calculate public pension from a defined benefits system over the full time path.

Used when the TPI solution evaluates taxes for all periods at once to compute aggregates. Each retiree’s benefit is computed from their own cohort’s wage and labor history: the wage entering the average for a household aged u at time t, earned at age s, is the wage at time t - (u - s). Histories that predate the time path use the period-0 wage (trend growth is handled by the de-trending in the benefit formula) and the model’s initial labor supply, so this reproduces exactly what the per-cohort household solves compute and household behavior and aggregates are consistent. The pension parameters are read at (t, u), the cell of the period-by-age arrays that belongs to the retiree’s own cohort. The inner work is vectorized rather than looped.

Parameters:

  • w_path (Numpy array) – real wage rate path, length T

  • e_ss (Numpy array) – effective labor units, size SxJ

  • n (Numpy array) – labor supply, size TxSxJ

  • n_preTP (Numpy array) – pre-time-path labor supply, size SxJ

  • S_ret (Numpy array) – retirement period by period and age, size T x S

  • S (int) – number of periods in the model

  • J (int) – number of lifetime income groups

  • g_y (scalar) – growth rate of technology

  • avg_earn_num_years (Numpy array) – number of periods earnings are averaged over, by period and age, size T x S

  • alpha_db (Numpy array) – replacement rate per year of contribution by period, age, and group, size T x S x J

  • yr_contr (Numpy array) – periods of contribution credited, by period and age, size T x S

ogcore.pensions.NDC_1dim_loop(w, e, n, S_ret, S, g_y, tau_p, g_ndc, t_birth, delta_ret, NDC)[source]#

Calculate public pension from a notional defined contribution system for one household over its lifetime.

The contribution made at working age s earns the rate credited in each later period up to the period before retirement:

\[\prod_{a=s+1}^{R-1}(1 + g_{NDC,t_0 + a})\]

where \(t_0\) is the household’s period of birth (age 0).

Parameters:

  • w (array_like) – real wage rate at each age, length S

  • e (Numpy array) – effective labor units at each age, length S

  • n (Numpy array) – labor supply at each age, length S

  • S_ret (Numpy array) – retirement period that applies to the household at each age u, length S

  • S (int) – number of periods in the model

  • g_y (array_like) – GDP growth rate

  • tau_p (Numpy array) – contribution rate the household faced at each working age s, length S

  • g_ndc (Numpy array) – calendar path of the credited rate (NDCRates.credit), a 1-D float array; periods outside it read the first or last element

  • t_birth (int) – model period in which the household is age 0

  • delta_ret (Numpy array) – conversion coefficient for the NDC pension amount that applies at each age u, length S

  • NDC (Numpy array) – pension amount for each household (filled in and returned), length S

Returns:

pension amount for each household

Return type:

NDC (Numpy array)

ogcore.pensions.NDC_2dim_loop(w, e, n, S_ret, S, g_y, tau_p, g_ndc, t_cross, delta_ret, NDC)[source]#

Calculate public pension from a notional defined contribution system for a cross section of S x J households in one period.

As in NDC_1dim_loop, contributions compound at the rates credited in the periods the household lived through: the household of age u in period t_cross was age s in period t_cross - u + s.
